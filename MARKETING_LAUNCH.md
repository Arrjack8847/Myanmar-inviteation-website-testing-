# Golden Autumn Heritage — practical launch kit

## Product
Mobile-first interactive wedding invitation websites, with a butterfly
opening, gently living garden, Burmese/English ceremony details, event
locations and countdown. Two currently working options share the same
mechanics: Golden Autumn Heritage and Moonlit Amethyst (a palette variant).

The browser-shot promo reels are generated by GitHub Actions:
`.github/workflows/create-marketing-reels.yml`. Download the two MP4s
from the `golden-autumn-two-skin-reels` artifact, review them and use
real captions/music licensed for your account. The reels contain sample
information only, with an explicit design-demo watermark.

## TikTok / Instagram Reels script — 12–15 seconds
0–2s: show the envelope and waiting butterfly. On-screen: "Not just a PDF invitation"
2–5s: tap to open, butterfly flight and wind-blown leaves.
5–8s: show the living garden and the couple's names.
8–11s: swipe through the family invitation and ceremony panels.
11–15s: show both skins. On-screen: "A wedding invitation that feels alive"
End-card text: "DM for your personalized invitation".

Suggested caption (English):
"Imagine opening your wedding invitation like a little love story. Animated
butterfly, immersive garden, personal names and all your celebration details,
made for mobile. Golden Autumn or Moonlit Amethyst? DM to discuss your design."

## First customer workflow
1. Share the two demo links and let the couple choose their preferred skin.
2. Collect their approved names, date/time with time zone, both families,
   invitation wording, event venues, map links and story text.
3. Create a separate customer deployment or copy of the template; edit only
   `wedding-config.js` and choose the skin. Do not overwrite another client's data.
4. Run `node tests/smoke.cjs`, `node tests/skin-system.cjs` and mobile QA.
5. Get the couple's written confirmation that all dates, map URLs, names and
   Burmese wording are correct **before** sharing the final URL.
6. Send the direct invitation link or a QR code made from the confirmed URL.

Do not claim false customer reviews, made-up bookings, or guaranteed delivery.
Set prices and revision scope based on your actual work before offering a package.
