- Run `node tests/smoke.cjs`.

- Ensure gateway and pathway actually align, typography remains clear, and the upper floral border doesn't obscure the names.
- Confirm no unintended duplicate gold crest: the high-res arch has an integrated central crest; this preview hides `08_gateway_carved_crest.png`.
- Check network payload and optimize images (WebP/AVIF) before publishing; the originals are large.
- Verify opening, leaf gust, butterfly flight, reduced motion and scroll. No backend or live deployment implied.
